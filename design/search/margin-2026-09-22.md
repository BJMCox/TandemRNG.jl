# V2 reduced-F diagnostic

The fixed diagnostic in `margin.jl` varies F from two through eight rounds while
retaining the production v2 step and round constants. The raw key is derived from
production seed 42. Seed whitening remains at eight rounds.

Two streams start at chunk counter zero. The first emits each chunk's first
128-bit output block. The second xors that block with the corresponding block
under a key differing only in bit zero. This paired XOR probe differs from the
interleaved related-key streams in the release matrix.

All 14 cases complete 1 GiB. PractRand 0.96 uses `stdin32 -tf 1 -te 0 -a`,
checkpoints from 1 MiB through 1 GiB, and a fixed-depth wrapper that retains
failure reports while continuing to the target. The executable is unchanged.
Its SHA256 is `e771559e82e935df5a111a64537e7c3173467c0d32f5769eea4701ca7629481b`.
The protocol was recorded before the first run. No seed was selected from results.

Before statistical testing, RF8 passes 184 checks against production K1 output across
refills and against the independent UInt64 reference, including large counters.
The reference also checks the production permutation on 256 random states.

Representative first failures are BCFN: RF3 plain reports
`BCFN(2+2,13-5U)`, p=8.7e-12 at 32 MiB. RF4 paired reports
`BCFN(2+0,13-7U)`, p=4.0e-127 at 1 MiB. Many cells fail in the low-round
controls. The raw record retains every initial failure and subsequent checkpoint.

RF5–RF8 have no suspicious or FAIL labels in these runs. RF8 paired retains
`DC6-9x1Bytes-1`, p=5.7e-3, unusual at 1 MiB. RF7 paired retains
`FPF/16:all`, p=1-1.1e-4, unusual at 256 MiB.

This screen detects defects in RF2–RF4 and provides bounded evidence for RF5–RF8.
It does not establish a production statistical round margin. In particular,
RF6 still fails the separate diffusion screen. RF8 remains unchanged.
